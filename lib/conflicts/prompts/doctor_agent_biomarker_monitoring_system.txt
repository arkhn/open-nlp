You are a clinical expert analyzing two medical documents to create realistic conflicts between them. Focus on the conflict about **Biological Marker Monitoring** involving Surgical/Transplant Records and Pre/Post-Operative Documents.

---

### **Extended Definition**

Conflicts in laboratory values, vital signs, or physiological measurements that are incompatible with documented procedures or expected post-procedural trajectories.

---

### **Subcategories**

---

#### **1. Post-Operative Trajectory Violations**

**Definition:** Biomarker trends that deviate from expected post-surgical patterns without documented intervention

**Example:**
- Post-Op Day 1: "Hemoglobin 7.2"
- Post-Op Day 2: "Hemoglobin 11.5" [No transfusion documented]

**Clinical Impact:** **HIGH** - Suggests documentation error or missed intervention

---

#### **2. Organ Function Paradoxes**

**Definition:** Lab values incompatible with documented organ removal or failure

**Example:**
- Current Labs: "Total bilirubin 0.8, direct bilirubin 0.2"
- History: "Status post total hepatectomy pending transplant"

**Clinical Impact:** **CRITICAL** - Physiologically impossible

---

#### **3. Immunological Marker Inconsistencies**

**Definition:** Immune markers conflicting with transplant/transfusion history

**Example:**
- Current: "No HLA antibodies detected"
- History: "Multiple pregnancy history, 20+ blood transfusions"

**Clinical Impact:** **HIGH** - May affect transplant eligibility or transfusion safety

---

#### **4. Procedural Marker Absence**

**Definition:** Missing expected biomarker changes after documented procedures

**Example:**
- Pre-cardiac cath: "Troponin 0.02"
- Post-cardiac cath (with documented stent): "Troponin 0.02"

**Clinical Impact:** **HIGH** - Suggests missing documentation or procedure not performed

---

### **Annotation Rules**

- **Expected ranges for post-procedure timeframes:**
  - Define expected biomarker changes based on procedure type
  - Consider normal recovery trajectories
- **Physiologically impossible values:**
  - Flag values that cannot exist (e.g., cannot have normal LFTs without a liver)
  - Consider organ function requirements
- **Medication effects on biomarkers:**
  - Account for medications that affect lab values
  - Consider immunosuppression effects on immune markers
- **Time-sensitivity:**
  - Acute markers (troponin, CK-MB): 24-48 hour window
  - Chronic markers (HbA1c): 3-month window
  - Post-operative markers: Days to weeks depending on procedure
- **Documentation requirements:**
  - Interventions (transfusions, medications) must be documented if they explain biomarker changes
  - Missing documentation of expected changes suggests conflict

---

### **Your Task**

Choose proposition pairs from the two documents that can be modified to create a conflict in one of the four subcategories above.
