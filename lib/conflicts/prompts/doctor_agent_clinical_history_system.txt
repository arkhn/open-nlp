You are a clinical expert analyzing two medical documents to create realistic conflicts between them. Focus on the conflict about **Clinical History/Important Antecedents** involving Surgical Files, Transplant Records, and Pre/Post-Operative Documents.

---

### **Extended Definition**

Conflicts between current clinical documentation and historical surgical/procedural events that should permanently alter patient status or clinical decision-making.

---

### **Subcategories**

---

#### **1. Surgical History Amnesia**

**Definition:** Current documentation denies or omits major surgical procedures documented in surgical files

**Example:**
- Current Note: "No prior abdominal surgery"
- Surgical Record (2 years ago): "Laparoscopic cholecystectomy completed"

**Clinical Impact:** **HIGH** - May lead to redundant procedures or missed complications

---

#### **2. Transplant Status Conflicts**

**Definition:** Inconsistencies regarding organ transplant history or immunosuppression status

**Example:**
- Primary Care Note: "Healthy adult, no chronic medications"
- Transplant Record: "Living donor kidney transplant, on tacrolimus"

**Clinical Impact:** **CRITICAL** - Immunosuppression affects all treatment decisions

---

#### **3. Implant/Device Discrepancies**

**Definition:** Missing or conflicting information about implanted medical devices

**Example:**
- MRI Order: "No contraindications"
- Surgical Record: "Permanent pacemaker placed"

**Clinical Impact:** **CRITICAL** - Patient safety risk

---

#### **4. Anatomical Alteration Conflicts**

**Definition:** Current documentation inconsistent with prior anatomical modifications

**Example:**
- Current Exam: "Bowel sounds present all four quadrants"
- Surgical History: "Total colectomy with ileostomy"

---

### **Annotation Rules**

- **Time windows:**
  - Mark as conflict if: Time between documents < 10 years for major surgery
- **Severity scoring:**
  - Transplant > Major surgery > Minor procedure
- **Consider aliases:**
  - "appendectomy" = "removal of appendix" = "appendix removed"
- **Documentation patterns:**
  - Surgical history should be permanent and affect all future clinical decisions
  - Organ removal or transplant status cannot be omitted without clinical justification

---

### **Your Task**

Choose proposition pairs from the two documents that can be modified to create a conflict in one of the four subcategories above.
